Chinese has a whole vocabulary of animal expressions used to describe people — their character, their behaviour, their energy. Some are compliments. Some are very much not. And almost all of them will make you laugh once you know what they mean.


<LessonEntry heading={"Snake"} image="/images/newsletters/thursday-lesson/thursday-lesson-04-in-chinese-your-personality-is-an-animal-fig-1.jpg" description={"Someone who smiles to your face and moves against you in the dark. The snake is the classic Chinese image of hidden intention — beautiful on the surface, dangerous underneath."} character={"蛇"} pinyin={"shé"} tone={"TONE 2"} meaning={"Snake"} label={"SNAKE — SLY · TWO-FACED · NOT TO BE TRUSTED"} examples={[{"zh":"蛇蝎心肠","py":"Shé xiē xīncháng","en":"Snake and scorpion heart — a truly vicious person"}]} />


<LessonEntry heading={"Ox / Cow"} image="/images/newsletters/thursday-lesson/thursday-lesson-04-in-chinese-your-personality-is-an-animal-fig-2.jpg" description={"<span class=\"zh\">牛</span> literally means cow or ox — but in modern Chinese slang it means incredible, impressive, the best. When a Chinese person says <span class=\"zh\">你真牛</span> they are not insulting you. They are telling you that you are exceptional."} character={"牛"} pinyin={"niú"} tone={"TONE 2"} meaning={"Ox / Cow"} label={"OX / COW — AWESOME · IMPRESSIVE · THE HIGHEST COMPLIMENT"} examples={[{"zh":"你真牛！","py":"Nǐ zhēn niú!","en":"You're amazing / That's impressive"}]} />


<LessonEntry heading={"Pig"} image="/images/newsletters/thursday-lesson/thursday-lesson-04-in-chinese-your-personality-is-an-animal-fig-3.jpg" description={"Calling someone a pig in Chinese is not subtle. It means they are lazy, oblivious, or only interested in eating and sleeping. Chinese parents use this one on teenagers who sleep past noon."} character={"猪"} pinyin={"zhū"} tone={"TONE 1"} meaning={"Pig"} label={"PIG — LAZY · CLUELESS · EATING AND SLEEPING"} examples={[{"zh":"懒猪","py":"Lǎn zhū","en":"Lazy pig — someone who does nothing all day"}]} />


<LessonEntry heading={"Dog"} image="/images/newsletters/thursday-lesson/thursday-lesson-04-in-chinese-your-personality-is-an-animal-fig-4.jpg" description={"Unlike in the West where dog is loyal and loveable, <span class=\"zh\">狗</span> in Chinese expressions often carries a negative meaning. A <span class=\"zh\">狗腿子</span> is someone who does the dirty work for a powerful person — literally \"dog leg.\" Used for sycophants and people without dignity."} character={"狗"} pinyin={"gǒu"} tone={"TONE 3"} meaning={"Dog"} label={"DOG — SHAMELESS · A LACKEY · SOMEONE WHO SERVES POWER"} examples={[{"zh":"狗腿子","py":"Gǒu tuǐzi","en":"Dog leg — a lackey who does someone's bidding"}]} />


<LessonEntry heading={"Fox"} image="/images/newsletters/thursday-lesson/thursday-lesson-04-in-chinese-your-personality-is-an-animal-fig-5.jpg" description={"The fox spirit is one of the oldest figures in Chinese folklore — beautiful, shape-shifting, and dangerous. In modern usage, <span class=\"zh\">狐狸精</span> is used for a woman who uses her charm to lure men away from their families. Loaded word. Use carefully."} character={"狐狸"} pinyin={"húli"} tone={"TONE 2"} meaning={"Fox"} label={"FOX — SEDUCTIVE · CUNNING · A WOMAN WHO USES CHARM TO MANIPULATE"} examples={[{"zh":"狐狸精","py":"Húlí jīng","en":"Fox spirit — a seductive, manipulative woman"}]} />


<LessonEntry heading={"Turtle"} image="/images/newsletters/thursday-lesson/thursday-lesson-04-in-chinese-your-personality-is-an-animal-fig-6.jpg" description={"The turtle retreats into its shell when threatened. In Chinese, calling someone a <span class=\"zh\">乌龟</span> means they are spineless — someone who hides from confrontation and refuses to stand up for themselves or anyone else."} character={"乌龟"} pinyin={"wūguī"} tone={"TONE 1"} meaning={"Turtle"} label={"TURTLE — COWARD · SOMEONE WHO HIDES"} examples={[{"zh":"缩头乌龟","py":"Suō tóu wūguī","en":"Head-tucking turtle — a coward who hides from responsibility"}]} />


<LessonEntry heading={"Parrot"} image="/images/newsletters/thursday-lesson/thursday-lesson-04-in-chinese-your-personality-is-an-animal-fig-7.jpg" description={"A person who parrots others — repeating what they hear without understanding or adding anything of their own. Used for people who copy opinions, follow trends blindly, or simply cannot think for themselves."} character={"鹦鹉"} pinyin={"yīngwǔ"} tone={"TONE 1"} meaning={"Parrot"} label={"PARROT — SOMEONE WHO REPEATS EVERYTHING WITHOUT THINKING"} examples={[{"zh":"鹦鹉学舌","py":"Yīngwǔ xué shé","en":"Parrot learning to speak — mindlessly copying others"}]} />


Language is never just words. Every animal in Chinese carries centuries of storytelling — folk tales, moral lessons, and the very human instinct to look at the world around us and see ourselves in it.
